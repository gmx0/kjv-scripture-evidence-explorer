Versioning rule

Any change to formulas, component meanings, default weights, normalization, pruning, or tie-breaking requires a new algorithm version and a corresponding revision of this page.

Pipeline

  1. Preserve exact KJV text, case, offsets, and token position.
  2. Interpret the query as a reference, word, phrase, comparison, or modern topic.
  3. Generate separate candidate tiers from exact, phrase, morphological, letter-pattern, cross-reference, and topic-bridge evidence.
  4. Union and de-duplicate candidates.
  5. Calculate all applicable components and retain their explanations.
  6. Apply deterministic weights, rounding, and tie-breaking.

Order

The scorer constructs a deterministic optimal monotonic alignment, maximizing the corpus-derived rarity-and-length evidence of matched words rather than accepting the first repeated occurrence. Five measurements then recursively combine: weighted ordered coverage, relative-position agreement, pair-direction agreement, gap agreement, and adjacency continuity. Exact continuous order reaches the maximum. Same-order separated words decay according to their gaps. Reversed words retain weaker positive evidence through their shared wording and measurable pair-direction disagreement; they are not absolutely discarded.

Vocabulary and rarity

rarity(t) = [1 + ln((63,919 + 1) / (cf(t) + 1))] ÷ [1 + ln((63,919 + 1) / (1 + 1))]

cf(t) is the total KJV occurrence count. The observed frequency range, 1–63,919, supplies both bounds; even the most frequent word retains small nonzero evidence. The group weight comes from the 253 exact corpus occurrences of only: rarity can contribute at most 15.2318% of Textual, or 14.1341% of Final.

Rarity = recursive mean(word occurrence, word length prevalence, phrase occurrence, segment prevalence, letter-pattern prevalence, repetition prevalence)

Thus will contributes more rarity evidence than the when the measured corpus facts warrant it. No stop-word list removes common words. Crucially, these rarity measurements enter this one bounded group only: they do not also multiply Word, Letter, Order, phrase, or morphology scores.

Exact phrases as virtual words

phrase structure(p) = ln(1 + concatenated phrase letters) ÷ ln(19), bounded at 1
phrase rarity(p) = smoothed inverse exact displayed-phrase occurrence count

A continuous phrase of two or more exact words is scored as one additional exact virtual word under Word/Words only when its punctuation and spacing also agree, and that exact continuity additionally receives the 1.9868% Exact-Line component. Its structural length contributes to Word while its occurrence rarity contributes to Rarity. A phrase with the same token sequence but different punctuation is measured across token identity, surface forms, case characters, punctuation and spacing, and displayed characters—the same layers used for complete verses. It retains reduced Word evidence but receives no Exact-Line component.

Accordingly, one match for life is weaker than one for the longer and rarer everlasting; matching both separated words is stronger than either alone; and matching the exact continuous phrase everlasting life adds the virtual-word evidence and is stronger still.

Complete repeated verses

Complete token sequences are measured across token identity, surface tokens, exact-case characters, punctuation and spacing, and displayed characters. These layers recursively combine without fixed identity tiers. Exact repeated displayed text naturally reaches 1.000 because every measured layer is 1. The 119 exact repeated-text groups are regression-tested.

Shared segments and transformations

segment structure = length × geometric coverage of both forms × positional exactness
segment rarity = inverse prevalence at the observed position

The scorer records shared initial, terminal, and internal segments; it does not pronounce them roots, lemmas, affixes, compounds, or synonyms. Structural segment strength remains in Word; segment rarity is counted across all KJV vocabulary forms and enters the Rarity group once. Independent-token occurrence is reported as another fact, not a requirement.

Internal apostrophes and hyphens or dashes are treated symmetrically. The exact displayed form—such as man's or free-will—is retained as one form, while punctuation-delimited components are exposed at the same time. Thus man's may be compared through man, and free-will through free and will, without declaring either punctuation mark an absolute word boundary.

Accordingly, commanded ↔ revealed reports shared_terminal_segment [ed]. It remains positive, but its two-letter length, small coverage, and high corpus frequency make it weak. freewill ↔ selfwill receives substantially more for will.

Raw letter evidence

Lpair = coverage³ × [ln(1 + shared letters) ÷ ln(19)]
letter-pattern rarity = inverse measured fragment prevalence

Letter length is bounded by the observed 18-letter maximum. Fragment and character rarity enters the Rarity group instead of multiplying Letter a second time. One-letter overlap remains nonzero. Capitalization measures exact uppercase/lowercase positions and never discounts sentence-initial or allegedly grammatical capitalization.

Recursive combination without categorical vetoes

Each observable component remains visible. Within a group, components weight themselves by their measured values rather than hand-assigned percentages. Zero means the feature was not observed; short, common, distant, or differently capitalized observations receive small positive values rather than categorical rejection.

Candidate limits

Complete verse identities are admitted first. Remaining candidates are ordered before the 5,000-item quota by a corpus-bounded upper estimate from weighted exact-word coverage and rarity. The interface reports discovered, evaluated, and pruned counts.

Stable ordering

Results resolve by complete-verse identity, neighbour-weighted final score, textual score, canonical proximity, and finally canonical Bible order. This prevents geography from outweighing stronger wording while retaining every structural scale.

Interpretive boundary

The algorithm measures visible relationships in a versioned corpus. Similarity is evidence for study, not proof that two passages have the same meaning. Dictionary bridges and editorial cross-references remain separate from Scripture-derived evidence.
